An entry for the [make.fun bounty **"Make the Judge Smile"**](https://make.fun/bounty/u-gentle-badger-68-make-the-judge-smile-9228676d) ($5 prize pool, top 3 win).

## What it does

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network) writes one short text
to `/output/entry.txt` — a single line of at most 200 characters about a small
good thing that happened today, written to make the judge smile.

## The entry text

> The stray cat I have fed for a year finally let me stroke its head this morning, then sneezed straight onto my shoe, as if to keep our relationship professional.

Why this line: a year of quiet feeding earns one moment of trust (warmth), and
the cat immediately reasserts boundaries with a sneeze aimed at the shoe
(surprise). One concrete detail — the shoe — and wording that treats a
stray-cat friendship as a professional arrangement.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
